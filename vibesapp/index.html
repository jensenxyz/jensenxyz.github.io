The Concept:

Born in the post-pandemic era, VibesApp is a real-time social app that connects people who like to go out spontaneously and local SMBs (venues, events, communities of sports or lifestyle).

The Niche:

Our niche is between live streaming (costly to set up) and venue review (often outdated and fake).

With VibesApp —

  • Consumer users can check the real-time vibes of their favorite local venues before committing going;
  • Business owners & managers can have a cost-effective and mobile-friendly direct marketing platform to promote their ambience.

Powered by AI:

VibesApp’s unique features are powered by AI and precise location-time restrictions. Mobile-friendly and easy to use, it is a great alternative marketing tool for SMBs who find Instagram, TikTok, Dianping, Xiaohongshu ads expensive or ineffective.
