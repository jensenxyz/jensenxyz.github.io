Olifun

Find that happy hour nearby right now!

Co-founder · Orbit Startups accelerator, Batch #6

Olifun helps people discover happy hours and up-to-date events at the coolest bars and restaurants in megacities such as Shanghai, Beijing, and Shenzhen.

Its mobile app and chatbot leverage location-based services, social media, and mobile commerce to encourage spontaneity and bring people together.

Startup Accelerator · 2014

Accelerated by Orbit Startups

Olifun was selected for Batch #6 of Orbit Startups, one of Asia’s biggest startup accelerators. Going through the program as a co-founder taught me how to turn an idea into a real business, quickly and with evidence.

01

Lean validation

Testing assumptions with real users early, then iterating in short build–measure–learn cycles.

02

Business model & go-to-market

Shaping the value proposition, revenue model, and launch plan for both sides of the market: venues and their guests.

03

Pitching & storytelling

Sharpening the pitch with mentors and presenting Olifun to investors and partners.

04

Mentors & ecosystem

Learning from experienced founders and building a network across Asia’s startup ecosystem.

As a co-founder, I managed:

  • Product design (UI/UX). Creation from scratch.
  • Storyboard to user feedback and iteration.
  • User behavior analysis (in-person).
  • All-aspect operations including company entity setup, trademark registration, HR, and SOP.
  • Branding and marketing strategy.
